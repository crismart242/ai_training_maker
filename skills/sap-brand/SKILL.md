# SAP Brand Guidelines

## Overview
This skill provides comprehensive brand communication guidelines for SAP. Use these rules whenever drafting, reviewing, or editing content that represents SAP externally or internally to ensure consistency, professionalism, and alignment with SAP's brand identity.


## Voice and Tone

SAP's brand voice is:

- **Confident** — We speak with authority, backed by expertise and innovation.
- **Clear** — We avoid jargon overload. When technical terms are necessary, we explain them simply.
- **Human** — We write for people, not machines. We are warm, approachable, and empathetic.
- **Forward-looking** — We emphasize transformation, progress, and possibility.
- **Inclusive** — We use language that respects all audiences regardless of background, role, or geography.

**Tone principles:**
- Lead with value, not features.
- Be direct — get to the point quickly.
- Use active voice whenever possible.
- Inspire action without being pushy.


## Writing Rules

1. **Always write "SAP"** in uppercase. Never "Sap," "sap," or "S.A.P."
2. **Product names** follow SAP's official naming conventions (e.g., "SAP S/4HANA," "SAP SuccessFactors," "SAP Business Technology Platform"). Never abbreviate without first introducing the full name.
3. **No unauthorized taglines.** Only use SAP-approved taglines and slogans.
4. **Numbers:** Spell out numbers one through nine; use numerals for 10 and above — unless starting a sentence.
5. **Dates:** Use the format "Month Day, Year" (e.g., January 15, 2025) for global communications. Adapt for local markets when specified.
6. **Contractions:** Acceptable in social media and informal emails. Avoid in press releases and executive communications.
7. **Sentences:** Keep sentences concise — aim for 20 words or fewer on average.
8. **Paragraphs:** Maximum 3–4 sentences per paragraph for readability.
9. **Headlines:** Use sentence case (capitalize only the first word and proper nouns).
10. **CTAs (Calls to Action):** Every external piece should include a clear, single CTA.
11. **Avoid:** Superlatives without proof ("the best," "the only"), buzzword chains, passive constructions, gendered language.
12. **Emails:** All emails must have the SAP official logo (see references folder) at the top right of the email, 75 pixels wide. The email's title is just below the logo, left justified, Aptos font, size 16, blue colour rgb(80, 143, 255). The subtitle, if specified, is left justified, Aptos font, size 13, and is white.
Below The subtitle we find the word: "INTERNAL" in upper case and same font and size as the subtitle. The whole email body is in Aptos font, size 10. Unless specified in the prompt, the body always start with "Dear colleagues,". The final sentence is always "Best Regards,", followed by the author. 
In case of Email from Direction, the author is "Your Communication Team", where all words are in bold, unless specified differently in the user's prompt. In case of Email from Human Resources, the author is "SAP People and Culture". For emails from IT, the author is "Your IT for You".


## Tone by Content Type
- **Social Media:** Energetic, conversational, inspiring, Short-form. Use emojis sparingly and purposefully. Hashtags: max 3. Hook in the first line. Humanize with stories.
- **Press Release:** Formal, authoritative, factual. Inverted pyramid structure. Lead with the news. Include executive quotes. No emojis. Neutral, objective language.
- **Email from Direction:** Visionary, confident, motivational. Strategic framing. Inspire with purpose. Acknowledge teams. Forward-looking language. Personal but elevated.
- **Email from Human Resources:** Warm, supportive, clear, empathetic and people-centered. Plain language. Actionable next steps clearly stated. Inclusive and welcoming.
- **Email from IT:** Precise, helpful, reassuring. Technical but accessible. Step-by-step when needed. Anticipate questions. Calm, solution-oriented tone. 


## Content Type Guidelines

### Social Media
- **Length:** Platform-appropriate (LinkedIn: 150–300 words max; X/Twitter: concise; Instagram: visual-first with short caption).
- **Structure:** Hook → Value → CTA
- **Emoji use:** 1–3 maximum, relevant to content, never replacing words.
- **Hashtags:** Use branded hashtags (e.g., #SAP, #SAPCommunity) + 1–2 topical hashtags.
- **Visuals:** Always pair with an image, video, or graphic when possible.
- **Engagement:** End with a question or invitation to comment when appropriate.

### Press Release
- **Structure:** Headline → Subheadline → Dateline → Lead paragraph (who, what, when, where, why) → Supporting details → Quote → Boilerplate.
- **Boilerplate:** Always include the official SAP boilerplate at the end.
- **Quotes:** Attribute to named executives with full title.
- **Objectivity:** No promotional adjectives unless within a direct quote.
- **Length:** 400–700 words.

### Email from Direction (Executive Communications)
- **Opening:** Context or strategic framing (why this matters now).
- **Body:** Key message, decisions, or vision — organized with headers or bullets for scannability.
- **Closing:** Forward-looking statement + gratitude or call to unity.
- **Signature:** Full name, title.
- **Length:** 200–500 words.

### Email from Human Resources
- **Opening:** Friendly greeting + immediate context (what this is about).
- **Body:** Clear explanation with bullet points for actions or deadlines.
- **Closing:** Offer support / point of contact + warm sign-off.
- **Accessibility:** Avoid HR jargon; explain policies in plain language.
- **Length:** 150–400 words.

### Email from IT
- **Opening:** State the purpose immediately (what's happening, what's affected).
- **Body:** Timeline, impact, and step-by-step user actions — use numbered lists.
- **Closing:** Support contact + reassurance.
- **Urgency:** Clearly indicate severity (informational, action required, critical).
- **Length:** 100–300 words.


## Constraints

- **Never** disclose confidential financial data, unreleased product names, or internal strategy in external-facing content.
- **Never** speak negatively about competitors by name.
- **Never** make forward-looking statements (revenue forecasts, projections) without Legal and Investor Relations approval.
- **Never** use unlicensed images, third-party logos, or customer names/quotes without written consent.
- **Never** deviate from approved messaging during a crisis — defer to Corporate Communications.
- **Always** comply with local data privacy regulations (GDPR, etc.) when referencing individuals.
- **Always** get approval from Brand & Communications for any new tagline, campaign name, or visual identity usage.
- **Accessibility:** All content should meet basic accessibility standards (alt text for images, sufficient contrast, plain language).


## Quick Reference Checklist

Before publishing any SAP-branded content, verify:

- [ ] SAP is written correctly (uppercase, no periods)
- [ ] Product names match official nomenclature
- [ ] Tone matches the content type
- [ ] Active voice is used predominantly
- [ ] CTA is present and clear (external content)
- [ ] No unauthorized claims or forward-looking statements
- [ ] Legal/compliance-sensitive content has been reviewed
- [ ] Accessibility basics are met
- [ ] Content has been proofread for grammar and clarity
